# Generación de animaciones con vuvuzela

Herramienta: ImageGen integrada, cinco llamadas independientes con fondo transparente. Referencias: hoja original de cada jugador y `ejemplos/vuvuzela.webp`. Alfa original conservado. Las hojas se guardan en `src/data/players/player_N_vuvuzela.png`.

## office — player_1_vuvuzela.png

Use case: identity-preserve / sprite animation. Make ONE production sprite sheet for the existing side-scrolling beat-em-up Street Bogota. Image 1 is the exact CHARACTER AND ART STYLE reference; image 2 is the VUVUZELA OBJECT reference only. Preserve the reference character's face, hairstyle, body proportions, clothes, colors, chunky dark outlines and hand-painted arcade sprite style. Character: GOMELO: black coiffed hair, short dark beard, black sunglasses, navy blazer over unbuttoned white shirt, charcoal trousers, white sneakers.
Create EXACTLY NINE full-body sprites in a precise THREE COLUMNS by THREE ROWS equal cell grid on a truly TRANSPARENT background. NO text, numbers, borders, guides, floor or cast shadows. Each cell has ample empty padding so NO figures, hair, shoes or weapon touches another cell. All nine sprites same body scale, feet on identical cell-relative baseline, pelvis on same cell-relative horizontal pivot. Camera always side-on three-quarter facing RIGHT. Do not change direction between frames.
Every sprite firmly HOLDS THE SAME SMALL RIGID Colombian vuvuzela in the front hand: fingers visibly wrap around the red narrow handle; blue middle; yellow flared bell. The horn length is ONLY 45 percent of the character's full standing height, NEVER larger than half their body height. It stays the SAME size across all nine frames. Modest narrow bell no bigger than their fist to head. It is a short handheld melee club, NOT being blown into. No other weapons, phone, books, broom, mop or bucket.
READING ORDER left to right top to bottom:
cell 0: idle ready stance holding horn diagonally forward at waist, free fist near chest.
cell 1: armed walking cycle first stride right foot forward, keep horn gripped at waist.
cell 2: armed walking cycle passing feet, keep horn gripped at waist.
cell 3: armed walking opposite stride left foot forward, keep horn gripped at waist.
cell 4: armed walking second passing feet, keep horn gripped at waist.
cell 5: melee windup, front hand lifts horn beside shoulder, elbow bent, feet planted.
cell 6: start forward club swing, rotate shoulders and extend elbow, horn attached naturally to hand.
cell 7: decisive IMPACT pose, lunge and extend front arm toward RIGHT horizontally at chest height, horn pointing forward so yellow bell hits an enemy. Hand grips red end, blue and yellow extend ahead. Keep entire horn visible with plenty of right padding.
cell 8: follow-through recovery, elbow begins retracting the horn toward ready position, feet planted.
The full person AND horn must be an anatomically coherent single drawing in every cell. No separate floating horns or motion trails. Crisp readable silhouettes, consistent identity and scale, usable transparent alpha.
